## Gotchas
- Provider model lists can retain old aliases while promoting a new canonical
  ID; DeepSeek V4.1 Flash is `deepseek-flash`, not `deepseek-v4-flash`.
- Do not replace a vanished named model with an unrelated provider without a
  user-visible product decision.
